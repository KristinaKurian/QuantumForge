# Duskwing

**Category:** Starfighter

Duskwing is a compact Dominion starfighter optimized for fleet deployment and rapid interception. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Standard models sacrifice independent long-range capability in exchange for simplicity and numbers. They are usually launched from carriers, battle stations, or large warships and rely on central logistics.

Xarn Velgor uses a specialized high-performance Duskwing during the Battle of Helios Gate. His craft is more capable than the standard model and allows him to pursue Coalition fighters through the station's defensive corridor.
