# Battle of Helios Gate

**Category:** Event

Battle of Helios Gate is the Free Systems Coalition attack that destroys the first Void Core. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Using engineering plans carried by N7-KO, Coalition analysts identify a narrow vulnerability connected to the main reactor. Small Dawnlance fighters are used because larger vessels cannot enter the required attack corridor.

Lio Arken completes the decisive attack after relying on his awareness of the Aether Weave rather than only the targeting computer. The reactor chain reaction destroys the station before it can fire on the Coalition base.
