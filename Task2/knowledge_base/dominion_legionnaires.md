# Dominion Legionnaires

**Category:** Military

Dominion Legionnaires is the mass infantry forces of the Dominion of Orath. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Legionnaires serve aboard warships, battle stations, garrisons, and ground occupation units. Their equipment is standardized for logistics and intimidation rather than individual specialization.

They are supported by vehicles, walkers, aircraft, and orbital forces during major operations. Legionnaires participate in the search for stolen Void Core plans and the assault on the Coalition base during the Siege of Nivora.
