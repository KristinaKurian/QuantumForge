# Verdant Moon

**Category:** Moon

Verdant Moon is a forested satellite inhabited by small local communities that know the terrain extremely well. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

The Dominion builds a shield generator on the moon to protect the second Void Core while it is under construction nearby. Coalition commandos must disable this generator before the fleet can attack the station.

Local inhabitants initially distrust the outsiders but later assist the Coalition. Their knowledge of forest routes helps the strike team bypass Dominion patrols and reach the shield installation.
