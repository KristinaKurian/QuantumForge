# Pyrax

**Category:** Planet

Pyrax is a volcanic world covered by lava fields, industrial platforms, unstable rock, and extreme heat. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Near the end of the Replica Wars, Orin Vaal confronts his former student, who is already falling under Varek Sol's influence. Their duel takes place among dangerous industrial structures above lava flows.

The future Xarn Velgor is severely burned and physically disabled during the confrontation. Varek Sol retrieves him and places him in a sealed life-support suit, making Pyrax the symbolic beginning of Velgor's new identity.
