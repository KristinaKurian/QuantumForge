# Dawnlance

**Category:** Starfighter

Dawnlance is a single-seat Coalition starfighter used for interception, escort, and attacks against larger targets. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Dawnlance fighters carry energy cannons and compact guided weapons. Their wings change configuration between normal flight and combat mode, and their navigation systems can work with small onboard automatons.

Lio Arken flies a Dawnlance during the Battle of Helios Gate. The fighter's small size allows him to approach the Void Core's surface and reach the narrow attack corridor that larger ships cannot exploit.
