# Free Systems Coalition

**Category:** Organization

Free Systems Coalition is a decentralized resistance movement opposing the Dominion of Orath. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

The Coalition combines defected military personnel, local governments, smugglers, intelligence networks, and civilian supporters. Because it cannot match Dominion resources directly, it relies on mobility, hidden bases, intelligence, and attacks against specific strategic targets.

Mira Vey is one of its main political leaders. Lio Arken, Kael Rinn, and Brakk become prominent field participants. Major operations include Helios Gate, Nivora, and the attack near the Verdant Moon.
