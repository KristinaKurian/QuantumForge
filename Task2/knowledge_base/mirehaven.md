# Mirehaven

**Category:** Planet

Mirehaven is a remote swamp world with dense vegetation, shallow water, persistent fog, and few permanent settlements. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Its isolation makes Mirehaven suitable for hiding from Dominion surveillance. Eron Kai lives there after the destruction of the Lumina Wardens. Lio Arken travels to the planet after the evacuation of Nivora.

The difficult environment becomes part of Lio's training. He learns to distinguish immediate sensory impressions from deeper awareness through the Aether Weave while maintaining discipline under exhaustion.
