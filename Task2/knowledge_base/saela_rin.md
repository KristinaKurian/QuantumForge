# Saela Rin

**Category:** Person

Saela Rin is a former apprentice of the Lumina Wardens who leaves the organization before Directive Blackglass. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Because Saela is no longer formally part of the order, she survives the initial purge. She becomes an independent operative who protects civilians, investigates Dominion activity, and helps scattered resistance groups. She uses two shorter Prism Blades and favors mobility over direct power.

Saela supports many values of the old Wardens but questions their secrecy and political entanglements. Her experience makes her skeptical of rigid institutions even when she agrees with their goals.
