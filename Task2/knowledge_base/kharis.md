# Kharis

**Category:** Planet

Kharis is a dry outer-system world dominated by deserts, rocky settlements, moisture farms, and independent transport hubs. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Central authorities have historically had limited reach on Kharis. Smugglers, traders, local clans, and criminal groups use its settlements as meeting points far from the political center on Aurelia Prime.

Lio Arken grows up on Kharis and Orin Vaal hides nearby. The planet is also where Lio first meets Kael Rinn, Brakk, N7-KO, and PR-3X while the stolen Void Core plans are being moved.
