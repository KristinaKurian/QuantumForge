# Replica Guard

**Category:** Military

Replica Guard is genetically standardized soldiers created for the Helion Republic during the Replica Wars. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Replica Guards are organized into large formations and trained from an early stage for coordinated military service. Lumina Wardens frequently command Guard units during the war.

This close relationship becomes catastrophic when Directive Blackglass is activated. Most Guard units suddenly identify their Warden commanders as enemies, and the near-simultaneous timing prevents the Wardens from coordinating a response.
