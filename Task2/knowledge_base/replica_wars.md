# Replica Wars

**Category:** Event

Replica Wars is a large interstellar conflict fought during the final years of the Helion Republic. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Republic forces rely heavily on Replica Guards, while multiple systems and corporate factions support opposing armies. Lumina Wardens become military commanders even though their older role focused more on mediation and peacekeeping.

The prolonged war normalizes emergency powers and weakens oversight. Varek Sol secretly benefits from the conflict. The war ends with Directive Blackglass and the transformation of the Republic into the Dominion of Orath.
