# Dominion Rebellion

**Category:** Event

Dominion Rebellion is the prolonged conflict between the Dominion of Orath and the Free Systems Coalition. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

The conflict includes intelligence operations, local uprisings, fleet battles, evacuations, and attacks on strategic infrastructure rather than a continuous conventional front.

Three events define its later phase: the destruction of the first Void Core at Helios Gate, the withdrawal from Nivora, and the final attack near the Verdant Moon. The death of Varek Sol and destruction of the second Void Core cause central Dominion control to collapse.
