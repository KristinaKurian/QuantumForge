# Aether Weave

**Category:** Concept

Aether Weave is an energy field perceived by certain individuals throughout living systems. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Trained users describe the Aether Weave as both a source of information and a medium through which limited physical influence is possible. Associated abilities include heightened perception, telekinetic effects, sensing distant events, and influencing weak attention.

The Lumina Wardens teach restraint, awareness, and service. The Umbra Order instead emphasizes fear, anger, domination, and the conversion of intense emotion into immediate power.
