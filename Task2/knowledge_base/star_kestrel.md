# Star Kestrel

**Category:** Starship

Star Kestrel is Kael Rinn's heavily modified light freighter. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Although the Star Kestrel appears to be an aging cargo ship, it has unusually strong engines, upgraded navigation hardware, defensive weapons, and many nonstandard repairs. Kael and Brakk operate the ship together.

Its modified drive allows rapid transition into the Foldstream when the system is functioning correctly. Mechanical failures are common because components from different generations have been combined, so Brakk's engineering skill is essential.
