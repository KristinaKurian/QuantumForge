# Foldstream

**Category:** Technology

Foldstream is the faster-than-light travel regime used by interstellar ships. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

A vessel equipped with a compatible drive can leave ordinary space and cross distances that would otherwise require years. Navigation must be calculated carefully because gravitational hazards still affect safe routes.

Navigation automatons such as N7-KO can calculate transitions faster than most biological crews. Damage to a Foldstream drive can leave a ship trapped in ordinary space and vulnerable during pursuit.
