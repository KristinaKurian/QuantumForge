# Varek Sol

**Category:** Person

Varek Sol is the ruler of the Dominion of Orath and the senior master of the Umbra Order. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Sol gains power by presenting himself as the solution to instability during the Replica Wars. Once emergency authority is concentrated in his office, he replaces the Helion Republic with the Dominion and orders Directive Blackglass against the Lumina Wardens.

Varek Sol prefers manipulation over open confrontation. He tries to turn Lio Arken by forcing him into conflict with Xarn Velgor. During the final battle, Velgor rejects Sol's command and kills him to save Lio.
