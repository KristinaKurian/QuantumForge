# Umbra Order

**Category:** Organization

Umbra Order is a secretive tradition of Aether Weave users who seek power through domination, fear, and controlled aggression. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Varek Sol is the senior Umbra master during the fall of the Helion Republic. Xarn Velgor becomes his principal enforcer after the duel on Pyrax.

Unlike the Lumina Wardens, the Umbra Order treats political institutions mainly as instruments. Sol uses public office, military conflict, and emergency powers to create the Dominion while concealing his deeper role.
