# Lio Arken

**Category:** Person

Lio Arken is a pilot from the desert world Kharis who later becomes a trainee of the Lumina Wardens. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Orin Vaal first teaches Lio about the Aether Weave and gives him a Prism Blade. Eron Kai later continues his training on Mirehaven. Lio is also a gifted pilot and destroys the first Void Core during the Battle of Helios Gate by exploiting a narrow reactor vulnerability.

Lio is the son of Xarn Velgor and the twin brother of Mira Vey. Instead of treating Velgor only as an enemy, Lio believes his father can reject the Umbra Order. That belief becomes decisive during the battle above the Verdant Moon.
