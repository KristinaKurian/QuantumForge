# Kael Rinn

**Category:** Person

Kael Rinn is an independent pilot and smuggler who becomes a senior field operative of the Free Systems Coalition. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Kael pilots the Star Kestrel, a heavily modified light freighter optimized for speed, evasive maneuvers, and improvised repairs. At first he accepts dangerous transport work mainly for payment, but repeated contact with Dominion violence changes his priorities.

Brakk is Kael's co-pilot and closest friend. After helping Lio Arken and Orin Vaal escape Kharis, Kael joins major Coalition operations, including the Battle of Helios Gate, the evacuation of Nivora, and the mission near the Verdant Moon.
