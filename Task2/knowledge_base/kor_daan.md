# Kor Daan

**Category:** Person

Kor Daan is a Veydran bounty hunter known for tracking difficult targets across remote systems. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Kor uses modular armor, a compact jet system, sensors, restraint tools, and ranged weapons. He is not a formal member of the Dominion military, but accepts Dominion contracts when they match his interests.

Xarn Velgor hires Kor Daan during the search for Kael Rinn and the Star Kestrel. Kor's strength is patience: he studies routes, fuel use, contacts, and likely escape points before approaching a target.
