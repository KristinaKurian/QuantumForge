# Prism Blade

**Category:** Technology

Prism Blade is a close-combat energy weapon associated primarily with the Lumina Wardens and the Umbra Order. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

A focused energy field forms a short luminous blade from a compact hilt. The weapon can cut many common materials and redirect some energy projectiles, so effective use requires precision and disciplined awareness.

Wardens combine physical training with perception through the Aether Weave. Individual users build personalized hilts and may choose different configurations; Saela Rin, for example, carries two shorter blades.
