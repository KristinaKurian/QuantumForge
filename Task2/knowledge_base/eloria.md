# Eloria

**Category:** Planet

Eloria is a prosperous world known for education, diplomacy, and civic institutions. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Mira Vey is raised on Eloria and represents its political traditions within the Free Systems Coalition. The Dominion later destroys the planet with the main weapon of the Void Core after demanding information about the Coalition.

The destruction of Eloria demonstrates that the Void Core is an instrument of political terror, not merely a military base. The attack becomes a major recruiting event for the Coalition and changes many systems' attitude toward resistance.
