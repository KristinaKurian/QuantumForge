# Dominion of Orath

**Category:** Organization

Dominion of Orath is the authoritarian state created when Varek Sol replaces the Helion Republic after the Replica Wars. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

The Dominion centralizes military, legal, and administrative authority on Aurelia Prime. Regional autonomy is reduced, opposition is criminalized, and Dominion Legionnaires are deployed across occupied systems.

Projects such as the Void Core are intended to reduce dependence on conventional occupation by making resistance appear hopeless. The destruction of Eloria has the opposite effect and strengthens support for the Free Systems Coalition.
