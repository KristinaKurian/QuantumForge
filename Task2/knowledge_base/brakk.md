# Brakk

**Category:** Person

Brakk is a Korran mechanic, navigator, and co-pilot of the Star Kestrel. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Brakk is physically powerful but is also an experienced engineer. He can repair propulsion, shield controls, power routing, and navigation systems under combat conditions. His language is based on growls and tonal calls that Kael Rinn understands fluently.

Brakk's loyalty to Kael comes from years of shared work rather than formal military service. When Kael joins the Free Systems Coalition, Brakk follows and becomes important in the evacuation from Nivora and the operation near the Verdant Moon.
