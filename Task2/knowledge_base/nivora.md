# Nivora

**Category:** Planet

Nivora is an ice-covered world used by the Free Systems Coalition as a temporary hidden base. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Harsh weather and remote geography initially make Nivora difficult for the Dominion to survey. Once the base is discovered, Dominion forces launch a large ground assault supported by heavy armored walkers.

Coalition forces delay the attackers while transports evacuate personnel and equipment. The Siege of Nivora is not a Coalition victory, but the withdrawal preserves much of its leadership. Lio Arken then travels separately toward Mirehaven.
