# Eron Kai

**Category:** Person

Eron Kai is an ancient master of the Lumina Wardens who hides on the swamp world Mirehaven. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Eron trains Lio Arken in concentration, perception, and disciplined use of the Aether Weave. He repeatedly warns that fear and anger can make a person vulnerable to the methods of the Umbra Order.

Eron considers Lio's decision to confront Xarn Velgor extremely dangerous because of their family connection. Before dying, he confirms that Velgor is Lio's father and hints that another close family member survives.
