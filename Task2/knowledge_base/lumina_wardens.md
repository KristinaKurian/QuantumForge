# Lumina Wardens

**Category:** Organization

Lumina Wardens is an ancient order of Aether Weave users who serve as mediators, investigators, and defenders of the Helion Republic. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Wardens train in awareness, negotiation, physical discipline, and use of the Prism Blade. Their doctrine warns against using fear and anger as primary sources of power.

The order becomes deeply involved in military command during the Replica Wars, weakening its independence. Varek Sol then isolates it politically. Most Wardens are killed during Directive Blackglass, although Orin Vaal, Eron Kai, and Saela Rin survive.
