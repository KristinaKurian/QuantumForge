# Mira Vey

**Category:** Person

Mira Vey is a political leader and field commander of the Free Systems Coalition who was raised on Eloria. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Mira coordinates diplomacy, intelligence, and military operations. After the Coalition base on Nivora is discovered, she helps organize the evacuation while Dominion forces advance across the ice fields. She is known for balancing political goals with practical operational decisions.

Mira is Lio Arken's twin sister and Xarn Velgor's daughter, although she does not know this early in the conflict. She later shows sensitivity to the Aether Weave but remains focused on rebuilding democratic institutions after the Dominion collapses.
