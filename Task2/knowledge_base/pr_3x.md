# PR-3X

**Category:** Automaton

PR-3X is a humanoid protocol automaton designed for translation, etiquette, and communication between cultures. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

PR-3X understands a very large number of biological and machine languages. It travels with N7-KO during the attempt to deliver the Void Core plans to the Free Systems Coalition. Its cautious personality often conflicts with N7-KO's improvisation.

PR-3X is not designed for combat, but translation and cultural knowledge repeatedly become operationally important. It also interprets Brakk's Korran language when other team members cannot understand him.
