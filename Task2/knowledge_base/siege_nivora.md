# Siege of Nivora

**Category:** Event

Siege of Nivora is the Dominion assault that begins after it discovers the Free Systems Coalition's hidden base on Nivora. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Dominion ground forces use heavy armored walkers to advance on shield and power systems. Coalition defenders are ordered to delay the assault rather than hold the planet indefinitely.

Most transports escape with personnel and essential equipment. Kael Rinn and Mira Vey leave aboard the Star Kestrel, while Lio Arken travels separately toward Mirehaven after receiving guidance about Eron Kai.
