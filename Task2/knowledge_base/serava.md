# Serava

**Category:** Planet

Serava is a temperate world of lakes, wetlands, old cities, and strong political traditions. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Serava plays an important diplomatic role during the final decades of the Helion Republic. Varek Sol begins his public political rise through crises connected with the planet and builds a reputation as a defender of stability.

Behind that public role, Sol manipulates rival groups and institutions. Serava matters less as a battlefield than as the place where political conditions are created for his later concentration of power.
