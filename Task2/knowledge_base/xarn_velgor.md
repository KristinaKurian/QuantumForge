# Xarn Velgor

**Category:** Person

Xarn Velgor is a former Lumina Warden who became the principal enforcer of Varek Sol and the Dominion of Orath. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

After catastrophic injuries on Pyrax, he survives inside a sealed black life-support suit. Velgor is an exceptional Prism Blade fighter and a powerful user of the Aether Weave. He hunts surviving Wardens, suppresses Coalition cells, and protects strategic Dominion projects, including the Void Core program.

Xarn Velgor is the father of Lio Arken and Mira Vey, although that relationship is hidden for years. During the final confrontation above the Verdant Moon, Lio refuses to kill him. Velgor finally rejects Varek Sol and saves Lio, sacrificing his own life.
