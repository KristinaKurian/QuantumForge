# Aurelia Prime

**Category:** Planet

Aurelia Prime is a planet covered by an immense multi-level city and the political center of the Helion Republic and later the Dominion. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Aurelia Prime contains government districts, transport layers, industrial zones, archives, and the former central sanctuary of the Lumina Wardens. Decisions made there affect thousands of inhabited systems.

During the final years of the Republic, Varek Sol gradually centralizes emergency powers on Aurelia Prime. After Directive Blackglass, Dominion forces occupy the Warden sanctuary and the planet becomes the administrative heart of the new regime.
