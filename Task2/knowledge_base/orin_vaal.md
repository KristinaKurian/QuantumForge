# Orin Vaal

**Category:** Person

Orin Vaal is a former Lumina Warden who survives Directive Blackglass and hides on Kharis. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

Orin watches over Lio Arken from a distance until the Dominion begins searching for stolen Void Core plans. He introduces Lio to the Aether Weave, teaches patience and restraint, and gives him his first Prism Blade.

During a rescue operation aboard the Void Core, Orin confronts Xarn Velgor. He deliberately allows the confrontation to end his physical life so that the others can escape. Lio later experiences Orin as a guiding presence through the Aether Weave.
