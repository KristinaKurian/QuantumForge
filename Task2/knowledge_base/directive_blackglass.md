# Directive Blackglass

**Category:** Event

Directive Blackglass is the secret command used by Varek Sol to destroy the Lumina Wardens near the end of the Replica Wars. This article belongs to the fictional Astraforge knowledge base and is self-contained so that the entity can be understood without relying on an external fictional universe.

When the directive is activated, Replica Guard units across many systems abruptly treat their Warden commanders as hostile targets. The distributed timing prevents the order from organizing a coordinated defense.

Varek Sol later describes the Wardens as traitors and uses the purge to justify further centralization. A small number survive because they escape, are isolated from Guard units, or are no longer formal members of the order.
